1. SELECTED COMPOSITION — WHY B
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The final is Concept B, refined: a soft key light on her face, a larger
foreground passport, and slightly larger type on the tape.

Why it wins:
  1. Message in one second: the lit checkpoint sign with its downward arrow
     sits directly above her head. "Border / control / departure" reads before
     a single word does, and it creates a natural halo that separates her
     dark hair from the background.
  2. Tension without false claims: the ATTENTION tag, the navy/white
     hatching, the chevrons and the "CONTRÔLE DES PASSEPORTS" tape create
     urgency through geometry and light only. No legal statement, no figure.
  3. Clear hierarchy: face → QUITTER LA FRANCE ? → ATTENTION → passport.
     Each element has a role; at 320x180 the headline and the face hold.
  4. Brand: strictly navy / near-black / white. The hatching and the square
     tags echo the geometry of the FYNOVATES symbol.

Why not the others:
  A is the most "cinematic departure" (monumental terminal, departure board,
    aircraft), but at small size the terminal reads as a generic dark
    background: less tension, and the airport is less recognisable.
  C has the best wealth/finance story (business district, route leaving the
    passport), but the route and the skyline compete with the headline and the
    airport is barely identifiable. It's more abstract and less immediate.

2. THE THREE DIRECTIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
A — THE INTERNATIONAL DEPARTURE
  Night terminal: steel roof structure, curtain wall, aircraft on the apron,
  floodlights, departure board behind her. Open passport with visa stamps in
  the lower-left foreground, boarding pass in soft focus behind it. Enormous
  headline + "AVANT DE PARTIR…" tag.
B — THE IMMIGRATION WARNING
  Passport-control hall: glass booths in perspective, queue barriers, lit
  signs with arrows, a diagonal light beam. ATTENTION tag, chevrons,
  "CONTRÔLE DES PASSEPORTS" tape, passport + boarding pass.
C — THE GLOBAL FINANCIAL EXIT
  International business district seen through the terminal glass. A dotted
  luminous route leaves the visa stamps and climbs toward the business
  district. "AVANT DE PARTIR…" tag.

4. QUALITY CONTROL
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  [x] Spelling: "QUITTER LA FRANCE ?" with a narrow no-break space before "?",
      "AVANT DE PARTIR…", "ATTENTION", "CONTRÔLE DES PASSEPORTS" (circumflex
      checked).
  [x] Authentic logos, proportions locked, top-left, subordinate to the headline.
  [x] Palette: plates gradient-mapped to #0F0A13 → #0D082E → white; tags and
      tape in #0D082E / #FFFFFF. No red, yellow, orange, green or gold.
  [x] Portrait: natural, undistorted, face never covered; shading and key
      light applied as overlays masked to her silhouette (reversible in HTML).
  [x] Documents: no official seal, no coat of arms, no real country, no
      personal information, no claimed visa or approval.
  [x] 1280 x 720 checked on all 4 PNGs (x2 supersampling, then Lanczos).
  [x] 320 x 180 previews generated and reviewed (editable_source/previews/).
  [x] Bottom-right corner (YouTube duration badge) carries no key information.
  [!] Generated props: the stamps show only fictional dates; the boarding
      pass shows lines instead of text. Check before publishing that this
      suits your compliance policy.
